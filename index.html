HOW THE IDEAS FIT TOGETHER

Three ways to understand
the same matrix.

The four fundamental spaces describe what a matrix can produce and what it annihilates. Rank counts the independent directions. SVD then chooses orthonormal coordinates that make the action of the matrix transparent.
